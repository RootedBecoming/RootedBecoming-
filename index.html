Desert Marigold

Baileya multiradiata is a yellow wildflower native to arid regions of the southwestern United States. It is adapted to dry conditions and often blooms after seasonal rains.

Observe its flowers, leaves, habitat and season. Never use an app lesson alone to identify a wild plant for eating or medicinal use.

Our Promise

The app is a tool, not a destination.

Rooted Becoming helps people navigate life's transitions with self-awareness, practical skills, emotional resilience, meaningful learning and personal independence.

We measure growth by the life you build, not the time you spend here. The goal is to need the app less as your confidence and independence grow.
